Pigeon Support

Pigeon keeps count of what you eat, drink and do.

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email pigeonlogeverything@gmail.com. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps with a bug.

Questions

How do I log something?

Tap + at the top of Home, then tap an item to log one serving. Undo takes it back. To log several at once, type what you ate, drank or did, like "two eggs, a slice of toast and a coffee", and tap Estimate.

What are items and loggables?

Loggables are the totals you keep, like Caffeine in mg. Items are what you log, like Coffee: each serving adds set amounts to your loggables. Make your own, or add ready-made ones from the ideas in the Items and Loggables tabs.

How do I change or delete a log?

Tap the log's name in Home's timeline.

How do I keep a loggable first on Home?

Pin it: hold its name at the top of Home's timeline and tap Pin, or swipe right on it in the Loggables tab.

Why doesn't Estimate offer AI?

AI needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Pigeon reads what you typed with its own rules, on your iPhone.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say "Log Coffee in Pigeon", or "Get my Caffeine total in Pigeon". Pigeon's actions are also in the Shortcuts app.

Do my logs sync to my other devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when you're signed in to iCloud. Without it, your logs stay on this iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove your logs from your iPhone. To remove the copy in your iCloud too, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, choose Pigeon and delete its data.
