Using Pigeon

How do I log something?

Tap + on Home, then tap an item. To log several at once, type them out, such as "two eggs, a slice of toast and a coffee", and tap Estimate.

What are items and loggables?

A loggable is a running total, such as Caffeine in mg. An item is something you log, such as Coffee; each serving adds to its loggables.

How do I edit or delete a log?

Tap its name on Home.

How do I pin a loggable?

Hold its name on Home and tap Pin. Pinned loggables stay first.

Why isn't AI offered in Estimate?

It requires an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Does Pigeon work with Siri?

Yes. Try "Log Coffee in Pigeon" or "Get my Caffeine total in Pigeon". Its actions are also in the Shortcuts app.

Data and Sync

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes, through your iCloud account. Without iCloud, data stays on the device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Pigeon.

What does Pigeon collect?

Nothing. See the Privacy Policy.
