Pigeon Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Pigeon collects no data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server your data could reach.

Where your data is kept

  • Your items, loggables and logs are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, Apple syncs them to your other devices through your private iCloud database. Apple keeps that copy under your Apple Account and its own privacy policy. We can't read it.

Estimate

  • Pigeon reads what you type with its own rules, on your iPhone.
  • If you choose AI, on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, what you typed goes to Apple's language models, which run on your iPhone or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. It never reaches us.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Pigeon from Siri or Shortcuts, Apple handles your request under its own privacy policy. Pigeon only logs an item or reads a total, on your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share any of your data.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • Pigeon includes no third-party code that collects data, such as analytics or ad networks.
  • Pigeon asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Health data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove your logs from your iPhone. To remove the copy in your iCloud too, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, choose Pigeon and delete its data.

Children

Pigeon isn't meant for children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
