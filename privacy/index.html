Details

Your data

Everything you enter is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, Apple syncs it through your private iCloud database. We can't access it.

Estimate

Text you enter is read on your device. If you choose AI, Apple Intelligence processes it on the device or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. It's never sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Pigeon.

Children

Pigeon is not directed at children under 13.
